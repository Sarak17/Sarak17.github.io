In Development

ATLAS

Atmospheric Threat & Localised Alert System

An early-stage web application exploring how historical tornado records could be presented as simple, regional risk information. The current prototype shows an interactive map of the United States, with a risk score and a list of past tornado events for each state.

It currently runs on example data only, so its risk scores are illustrative rather than results. It is structured so that a real historical record, such as NOAA’s Storm Events data, could be connected later. It does not issue alerts or forecasts.
