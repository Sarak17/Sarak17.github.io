    About me

    Biology, measured carefully.

    My professional grounding is in molecular diagnostics, and my research focus is cancer spatial omics. I want to understand disease by connecting what happens in the laboratory with what quantitative analysis can reveal.

    I also care about the wider context of science, which led me to start Enviroclime, an initiative where scientific thinking meets environmental sustainability.
